Getting There

The technical address is 1201 B Boscobel St., Nashville, TN 37206 — but you'll actually arrive from the alley behind the house.

You will access the building from the alley behind our residence. Enter the alley off S. 11th Street, between Boscobel Street and Lillian Street — it dead-ends just beyond our property, so it isn't a thruway. You can't reach the alley from 14th St., the next cross street over.

GPS tip: the alley doesn't show up on some map apps. The easiest way to find it is to navigate to 315 S. 11th St., Nashville, TN 37206 — the alley entrance is just to the left of that address. We're the last garage on the right side, right before the alley dead-ends.

The alley is quiet and well lit near our place.

There's no walkway through our property, and a steep grassy hill sits behind the house — please don't park or get dropped off on the street out front.
Parking

Your dedicated guest spot is in the gravel to the right of the building, right beside the gold door — easy for unloading luggage. If you're arriving by Uber/Lyft/Taxi, have your driver pull into that gravel area if they need extra time, and please be mindful of neighbors sharing the alley.

Check-in & Door Code

We'll text you a 4-digit access code on the day of arrival, close to 3:00 PM check-in. Enter the code on the keypad — you'll hear the lock release once it's done. In the dark, press the lock icon to light up the numbers.

To lock up from inside, turn the deadbolt. From outside, just close the door fully and press the lock icon — you'll hear it engage.

Five Points
11th, Woodland & Clearview — the neighborhood hub

Head out the alley and take a right on S. 11th Street — a few restaurants and shops sit at 11th & Fatherland. Continue a couple more blocks and you're at Five Points, the neighborhood's hub for restaurants, bars, live music, and Bongo East coffee, now sharing its space with Game Point Cafe's 700+ board games. Five Points Pizza anchors the corner. A few blocks up at Woodland & 14th, you'll find The Lipstick Lounge, which recently opened a sister sports bar next door called Chapstick.

Keep going down 11th and you'll hit Gallatin Road (more restaurants, bars, pharmacies, groceries), or head to 10th Street for Hunter's Station, a food hall home to Hugh Baby's, Vui's Kitchen, Everbowl, Music City Gyros, Kisser, and more.

Coffee Maker & Tea

Our Hamilton Beach machine brews a full pot, or single cups via Keurig-style K-cups or loose grounds — there's also a French press on the shelf above, plus a few K-cup and tea options on hand.

Single-serve K-Cup
  • Select the single-serve button.
  • Pour the amount of water you want brewed into the reservoir on the right side (10 oz. max).
  • Add your K-cup and make sure the lid clicks down.
  • Choose your brew strength, then press Brew Now to start.
  • Press Brew Now again when it's done, and rinse the pack holder after use.
Single-serve with grounds
  • Plug in, lift the lid latch, and open the brew basket lid.
  • Add ground coffee to the brown basket (3 tbsp max), close it, and set it back in the funnel.
  • Close the main lid. Fill your mug (14 oz. max) and add that amount of water to the reservoir.
  • Press Brew Now to start, then again to turn off and unplug. Rinse the basket after use.
Full pot / carafe
  • Plug in and press the carafe button. Lift the lid and remove the carafe brew basket.
  • Add a paper filter and one tablespoon of grounds per cup, then set the basket back in place.
  • Fill the carafe with up to 12 cups of cold water and pour into the reservoir. Close the lid.
  • Set the carafe (lid on) on the warming plate, choose brew strength, and press Brew Now.
  • Press Brew Now again to turn off and unplug. Rinse the basket and carafe after use.
Washing Machine

We have a Whirlpool washer with a Load & Go feature — it holds a supply of high-efficiency (HE) detergent and automatically doses each load based on the weight of your clothes. Just make sure the Load & Go button on the right is lit up, choose your wash settings, and press Start.

If you'd rather use your own detergent, turn off the Load & Go button and use the machine like a standard washing machine.
